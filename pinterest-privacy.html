Haq Content Manager
Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 13, 2026

Haq Content Manager is an owner-operated integration being developed for the HaqHeals Pinterest account. It is intended to help the account owner prepare content, schedule individually selected Pins and review account performance. It is not offered to unrelated Pinterest users.

The integration is not connected to the Pinterest API at present. The practices below describe how it will handle information if approved and authorized. This notice will be updated before any material change in those practices.

Information and its purpose

When the owner authorizes the integration through Pinterest, it will request only permissions needed to identify the owner's account and boards, publish selected Pins, reconcile publishing results and display relevant performance metrics. It will not request private messages, secret boards, billing information or other users' private data.

The integration will not collect Pinterest passwords. Authorization credentials will be kept on the owner's computer with restricted access, outside public repositories, public logs and AI prompts.

Content selection and AI assistance

The owner chooses each Pin, its destination and its schedule before publication. AI assistance may prepare original Haq content and draft descriptions. Pinterest API response data will not be sent to external AI services or used to train or improve AI models. The integration will not sell Pinterest data, use it for advertising elsewhere or automate likes, comments or follows.

Storage, access and retention

The intended integration processes Pinterest API responses transiently. It will not retain raw response archives. Storage of Pinterest-derived information will be limited to what Pinterest expressly permits or separately authorizes, and only for as long as needed for that permitted purpose. Original Haq media and owner-authored scheduling instructions will be maintained separately.

Access to application credentials and any permitted stored information will be restricted to the account owner and authorized operators who need it to administer the integration.

Pinterest, hosting and privacy requests

Publishing sends the selected content to Pinterest, which processes it under the Pinterest Privacy Policy. This notice is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process connection information, such as IP addresses and request details, as described in the GitHub General Privacy Statement. This page contains no forms, analytics scripts or advertising trackers added by Haq.

If you email a privacy request, Haq will use your email address and the information you provide to respond and handle the request. Email delivery is also processed by the email providers involved. Please do not send passwords or access tokens.

Revocation and deletion

The owner can revoke the integration's access through Pinterest and stop scheduled work. Privacy, access, correction and deletion requests can be sent to the contact below. Following revocation, termination or another applicable deletion trigger, the operator will stop affected processing and complete required deletion of Pinterest-derived information within Pinterest's applicable deadlines, including its 30-day outer limit where applicable. Original Haq media is handled separately.
