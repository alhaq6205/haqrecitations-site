About Haq

Haq Recitations is the publishing project behind the Haq family of Quran channels: HaqHeals, HaqEnglish, HaqUrdu, HaqSpanish and HaqRecitations. Our content includes Quran recitations, passages with translations, short videos and longer listening formats.

This website is maintained by the Haq account owner. HaqHeals is the public brand used for our Pinterest account and the account administered by HaqHeals Content Manager.

Explore HaqHeals on YouTube and HaqHeals on Pinterest.

HaqHeals Content Manager

HaqHeals Content Manager is an internal application being developed by the Haq account owner to manage content for the owner's HaqHeals Pinterest account. It is not a service for unrelated users or customers.

Its intended workflow lets the owner review each Pin's image or video, description, destination link, board and schedule before publication. Once authorized, the integration will publish those selected Pins, manage the owner's public boards, confirm publishing results and display permitted account performance information.

AI assistance may help prepare original Haq content and draft descriptions. Pinterest API response data will not be sent to external AI services or used to train models. The application will not automate likes, comments or follows.

Development status: Pinterest API access is not active. The application is undergoing review, and automated Pinterest publishing is not enabled.

Read the HaqHeals Content Manager Privacy Policy for information about proposed data use, storage, revocation and privacy requests.
